## English

**Short description**

```
Daily UPSC current affairs in English & Hindi, with quizzes and mock tests.
```

**Full description**

```
Unbound Daily turns the day's news into UPSC-ready notes, in English and Hindi.

Every morning you get short, clear explainers on the stories that matter for the Civil Services exam. Each one is tagged to the GS paper and subject, with key points, background and links to the original sources. An editor checks every article before it is published.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Daily current affairs: Polity, Economy, Environment, International Relations, Science & Tech, History & Culture, Geography and more
• English and Hindi: switch the whole app, articles and quizzes with one tap
• Daily quiz: 10 UPSC-style questions on the latest news, with explanations
• Weekly mock test: a timed Sunday test with UPSC marking (+2 / −⅔), your rank and percentile
• Practice by subject: work through questions on the topics you find hard
• Streaks and a weekly leaderboard to keep you going
• Bookmarks and notes for revision
• Offline reading for when you're on the move
• Audio briefing: listen to the day's top stories
• Smart reminders for the daily quiz and weekly mock (you choose which)
• Dark mode

FREE TO START
Read articles without signing up. Create a free account to take quizzes, track progress and save bookmarks. Premium adds the full archive, unlimited practice and bookmarks, offline reading and the audio briefing.

BUILT FOR ASPIRANTS
Content follows the UPSC syllabus and focuses on facts that get asked: schemes, reports, indices, constitutional provisions, places in the news and international agreements.

Unbound Daily is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with the Union Public Service Commission or any government body. Articles are prepared with the help of AI from public news sources and reviewed by editors; always confirm key facts from official sources.

Questions or corrections: write to us from the Contact page at unbound.zonty.in.
```
